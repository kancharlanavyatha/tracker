SCIENTIFIC INTEGRITY ANALYSIS

Addressing Overfitting: Synthetic Formula Fit vs. Real-World Calibration

In early training iterations without noise, gradient-boosted trees and linear classifiers inverted synthetic generation formulas, resulting in artificially high scores (R2 > 0.99, Accuracy = 100%). In academic peer review and viva defense, such scores signal synthetic overfitting. To ensure robust scientific validity, we injected realistic biological entropy (daily hydration, stress, and sensor PPG noise) and benchmarked directly against genuine clinical human data.
